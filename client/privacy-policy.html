Smart Routine Scheduler

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 14, 2026

Smart Routine Scheduler ("Smart Routine," "the App," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Smart Routine Scheduler application.

Information We Collect

Smart Routine Scheduler does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal information from users.

The App does not require users to create an account or provide personal information to use its routine scheduling features.

Routine and Schedule Information

Users can create routines, schedules, and reminders within the App.

Routine and scheduling information is used only to provide the App's functionality. This information is stored locally on the user's device and is not transmitted to our servers, provided this accurately reflects the current version of the App.

Notifications and Alarms

Smart Routine Scheduler may request permission to send notifications and alarms.

These permissions are used only to:

  • Notify users when a scheduled routine or reminder is due.
  • Trigger alerts associated with routines created by the user.
  • Provide the core scheduling and reminder functionality of the App.

Users can manage notification permissions through their device settings.

Tracking and Advertising

Smart Routine Scheduler does not use user tracking technologies for advertising purposes.

The App does not sell user information or use personal information for targeted advertising.

Third-Party Services

Smart Routine Scheduler does not share users' personal information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Children's Privacy

Smart Routine Scheduler does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Security

Because Smart Routine Scheduler does not collect personal information on our servers, routine and scheduling information stored locally on the device remains under the user's control.

Users should maintain appropriate security protections on their devices.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the functionality or privacy practices of Smart Routine Scheduler change. Any updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
